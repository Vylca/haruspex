PC DOS (PCDOS-Regular.ttf, PCDOS-Bold.ttf)

The bitmaps are the ISO 9241-3 text mode fonts (8x16) of IBM PC DOS 2000,
code pages 437, 850, 852, 855, 857, 860, 861, 863, 866 and 869, as collected
by VileR in the VGA text mode font collection:
https://github.com/viler-int10h/vga-text-mode-fonts

Haruspex converts every pixel to a square outline (tool/build_font.py), adds
a bold variant and draws a few glyphs no code page has (typographic quotes and
dashes, the euro sign, the OE ligature and the bullets used by the editor).
